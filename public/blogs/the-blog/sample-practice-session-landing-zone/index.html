Landing Zone: A 40-Minute Chipping and Pitching Practice Session

Under Par Coaching Team · Updated November 27, 2026 · 2 min read

Great chippers don't aim at the hole. They aim at a landing spot and let the ball roll the rest of the way. This session trains exactly that, from the fairway and the rough. Even if you don't normally think about landing spots, it'll sharpen your trajectory and distance control.

What you need: a towel, a pile of balls, about 40 minutes and a short game area with fairway and rough.

The session at a glance

Block Time Reps Score
1. Warm-up 12 min 16 No score: find your rhythm
2. Landing Zone: fairway 15 min 24 Shots landing on the towel
3. Landing Zone: rough 15 min 24 Shots landing on the towel

1. Warm-up (12 min)

From the fairway, hit two shots each from 10, 15, 20 and 30 yards. Then do the same four distances from the rough. 16 shots.

2. Landing Zone challenge: fairway (15 min)

  1. Lay a towel 10 yards away. That's your landing zone.
  2. Hit 8 shots, trying to land each ball on the towel, not short of it and bouncing on.
  3. Move the towel to 20 yards, then 30 yards, and repeat.

Score: how many hit the towel at each distance, out of 8.

How to change trajectory: use a different wedge, or change your shaft lean at address and keep the same swing.

  • Hands ahead of the ball (forward press) → lower flight
  • Hands, shaft and ball in a straight line → higher flight

3. Landing Zone challenge: rough (15 min)

Same format: towel at 10, 20 and 30 yards, 8 balls each. Shots from the rough come out with less spin and roll out further, so you'll often need to land the ball shorter than you did from the fairway. Use the same trajectory adjustments.
