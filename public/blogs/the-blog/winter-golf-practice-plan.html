Winter Golf Practice Plan: Indoors, Simulators and the Gym

Under Par Coaching Team · Updated November 27, 2026 · 2 min read

The off-season is when good golfers get better. There's no score to protect, so you can make the swing change you've been putting off, get stronger and build exact yardages. Here's a weekly plan for winter, whether you have a simulator, a garage net or just a hallway.

The winter week

Day Session Where
Mon Golf strength workout Gym or home
Tue Putting at home (20 min) Living room / putting mat
Wed Simulator or net: technique block Indoor
Thu Golf strength workout Gym or home
Sat Simulator: yardages and target games Indoor
Sun Mobility and putting (20 min) Home

Putting at home (20 min)

  • Gate drill (5 min): two coins or tees just wider than your putter head, 18 putts.
  • Start line (10 min): putt along a seam in the floor or an alignment stick, trying to roll the ball straight for 6–8 feet.
  • Distance ladder (5 min): putt to targets at 10, 15 and 20 feet on a mat or carpet. Stop each one within a putter length.

Simulator and net sessions

Technique block (45 min). Work on one swing change, one thought only. Half to three-quarter speed for the first 15 balls, then full speed. Film from face-on and down the line every 10 balls.

Yardages (first session, 45 min). Hit 5–7 full shots with every club, throw out the mishits, and record your average carry and your max. That's your yardage guide. Repeat for flighted wedges.

Target games (30 min). Pick a target and hit 10 balls with three different clubs. Score the average distance from the target. Simulators are perfect for this: they measure it for you.

Play a round. Simulate a course you'll play in the spring. Use your full routine on every shot and keep score honestly.

The gym

Winter is when you build the strength and mobility that turn into speed in April. Focus on the 5 muscle groups golfers need two or three days a week, plus a daily mobility routine for hips and shoulders.

When spring comes

Take the swing change to the course in casual rounds first, and switch to a scoring plan like the break 80 or break 90 program. The Under Par app has practice and fitness plans you can follow all winter, from $39/month.

Common questions

Can you get better at golf in the winter?

Yes. Winter is the best time for swing changes, fitness and building yardages on a simulator, because you're not worried about scores.

How should I practice on a golf simulator?

Treat it like a launch monitor, not a video game. Measure carries for every club, play scored target games, and simulate rounds on courses you'll play.
