Break 80 Practice Plan: What to Practice and How Much

Under Par Coaching Team · Updated November 27, 2026 · 2 min read

Golfers in the low 80s can usually hit the ball. What separates them from the 70s is wasted shots inside 120 yards: a wedge that comes up short, a chip to 15 feet, a 40-footer that turns into three putts.

So this plan spends most of its time close to the green, and keeps the driver accurate.

Where the strokes are

Area Goal for breaking 80
Tee shot Fairway or first cut, almost no penalties
Wedges Known yardages for every wedge, inside 20 feet from 100 yards
Short game Get up and down regularly from easy lies
Putting Lag to 3 feet, make nearly everything inside 4

The weekly schedule

Three sessions and one round. About 3½ hours of practice.

Day Session Time
Session 1 Save-par short game session 84 min
Session 2 Wedges and driver (below) 60 min
Session 3 Putting speed control 50 min
Weekend Play 18, log fairways, GIR, up-and-downs, putts —

Session 2: Wedges and driver (60 min)

1. Build your wedge chart (first week only, 20 min). Hit each wedge at full length, choked down 1 inch and choked down 2 inches. Write down the carries. Full method: wedge distance control.

2. Wedge ladder (25 min). Five shots each to 70, 85, 100 and 115 yards using your chart. Score: within 5 yards of the number. Use flighted wedges for at least half.

3. Fairway streak (15 min). Two flags as a fairway, driver until you miss. Score: best streak.

What to do on the course

  • Know three numbers on every approach: front, pin and back. Your yardage guide makes this easy.
  • Use the tee box. Tee up on the side that fits your shot shape.
  • Attack only with a wedge in hand. From mid-irons, play to the fat part of the green.

Measure progress every two weeks

Track up-and-down rate, putts per round and wedge shots inside 20 feet in practice. When those move, the scores follow. Want a coach to read the numbers and adjust the plan for you? That's Under Par Full Access (currently full, waitlist open).

Common questions

What is the hardest part of breaking 80?

Usually wedges and short game. Golfers shooting in the low 80s often hit the ball well enough; they lose shots inside 120 yards.

How many practice sessions a week do I need to break 80?

Three focused sessions plus a round is a good target. One of those sessions should be entirely inside 100 yards.
