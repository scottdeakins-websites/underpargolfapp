The 30-Minute Golf Practice Plan (3 Versions)

Under Par Coaching Team · Updated November 27, 2026 · 2 min read

You don't need two hours to get better. Thirty minutes with a target, a rep count and a score beats an afternoon of hitting balls without one. Here are three 30-minute sessions. Rotate them through the week.

Version A: Short game (30 min)

Drill Time Reps Score
Chips from the rough: 10, 15, 20 yds 10 min 5 each Inside 6 feet
Pitches from the fairway: 20, 30, 40 yds 10 min 5 each Inside 10 feet
Up-and-down game: drop 5 balls, chip and putt out 10 min 5 holes Number saved

The up-and-down game is the important one: every chip is followed by a putt that counts. Track your save rate week to week.

Version B: Range (30 min)

Drill Time Reps Score
Wedges to 80 and 100 yds 10 min 10 each Within 5 yds
Go-to iron at a flag 8 min 10 Inside 15 yds
Driver fairway streak between two flags 12 min Until you miss ×3 Best streak

Use your full pre-shot routine on every ball. Thirty minutes is only about 50 swings, so each one should count.

Version C: Putting (30 min)

Drill Time Reps Score
Gate drill from 3 ft 5 min 18 Clean through the gate
Circle drill: 3, 4, 5 ft around the hole 10 min 24 Makes
Lag: 30, 40, 50 ft 15 min 3 each × 2 Inside 3 feet

More drills in the full putting practice routine.

How to use them

  • Two days a week? Do A and C. Most strokes are saved close to the hole.
  • Three days a week? A, B, C.
  • Keep every score. Beat it next time.
