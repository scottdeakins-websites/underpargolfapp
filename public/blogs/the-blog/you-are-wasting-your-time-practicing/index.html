Are You Wasting Your Time Practicing? 5 Fixes

Under Par Coaching Team · Updated November 27, 2026 · 2 min read

You know the session. Swinging out of your shoes, trying to crush every driver, hitting 7-irons at nothing in particular until the divots line up perfectly. You leave feeling great. Then on Saturday it falls apart, because the course doesn't give you the same shot twice and there's no divot line to aim along.

Doing the same thing every week and expecting different results is the definition of insanity. Here are five fixes.

1. Plan practice in weekly blocks

Map out the week so every part of the game gets attention, not just whatever went wrong last round. Template: how to build a golf practice plan.

2. Don't chase last round's problem only

Bouncing from one fix to the next means nothing ever gets solid. Work on weaknesses, and keep building your strengths.

3. Practice your real pre-shot routine

Change clubs often. Put the club back in the bag between shots. Go through your full routine. That's what a round feels like.

4. Change your target every shot

Alignment on the course is hard because there are no lines on the ground. Don't let your old divots become your target line. Pick a new target for every ball.

5. Consistency beats intensity

Ten drivers on ten different days beats a hundred drivers in one afternoon. Short, regular sessions build skills that last. If you only have half an hour, use the 30-minute practice plan.
