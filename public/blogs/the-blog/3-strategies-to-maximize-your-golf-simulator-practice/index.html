3 Ways to Get More From Golf Simulator Practice

Under Par Coaching Team · Updated November 27, 2026 · 2 min read

A simulator isn't the same as hitting off real grass. But it's much better than not practicing, and a lot cheaper than a tee time at Pebble Beach. The key is to use it like a launch monitor and a golf course, not a video game. Three strategies:

1. Build your yardages

You can't pick the right club, or hit greens, without knowing how far each club goes. Use the simulator to record your carries for every club and build a yardage guide.

Lower handicaps should spend extra time on wedge distances in the off-season, including the choke-down method. Better wedge distance control shows up as more birdie chances.

2. Train shot shape

Block practice: spend a block hitting the shape that doesn't come naturally. Natural fader? Work on a draw. High ball? Work on flighting it down.

Tiger's 9-shot drill: with one club, hit nine shots in a row: a fade, a draw and a straight shot, each at high, normal and low trajectory. Nobody nails all nine. The point is clubface control, which is what gets you around a tree on Saturday. More on shaping shots in fade and draw basics.

3. Play golf on it

Treat it as a course, not a range. Play 9 or 18 holes, with your full pre-shot routine on every shot: talk through the shot, pick a target, commit.

Add consequences for misses, such as 10 push-ups or squats for every missed fairway or green. Your heart rate goes up, and that's the closest thing to tournament nerves you'll find indoors.
