Bobby Jones on Nerves: Work, Suffer and Wait for Fate

Under Par Coaching Team · Updated November 27, 2026 · 2 min read

Bobby Jones said nobody knows what will happen in golf until it happens. His answer to that uncertainty was simple: work hard, put up with the hard parts, and let fate do the rest.

The 1930 Grand Slam

That year Jones won all four of golf's biggest titles at the time: the British Open, British Amateur, U.S. Open and U.S. Amateur. It still hasn't been repeated.

He was nervous too

Jones was famously anxious during major championships, reportedly losing as much as 15 pounds over a major week. He also said nerves are what separate a thoroughbred from a plough horse. Feeling them means you care, and you're in a moment that matters.

What that means for you

  • Make your own luck. Preparation is what lets you play well when the nerves show up. Here's how to prepare for a tournament.
  • Build confidence, then compete. Put in the work, then enter the big event.
  • Don't let fear of nerves keep you out. Every big event you play makes the next one feel smaller. Need help choosing? See how to pick the right tournaments.
