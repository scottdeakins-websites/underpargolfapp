3 Things to Focus on for a Positive Mindset Next Round

Under Par Coaching Team · Updated November 27, 2026 · 2 min read

What's your mental focus for your next round? Most golfers don't have one. Pick one of these three and hold onto it for all 18 holes.

1. Only swing when you're committed

If you're standing over the ball with doubt, back off and start your routine again. A committed swing with the "wrong" club usually beats a doubtful swing with the right one.

2. Visualize as part of your routine

Before you step in, see the shot: start line, curve, height and where it lands. Make it a fixed step in your pre-shot routine, not something you do when you remember.

3. Stay on the next shot

Not the double on the 4th, not the score you need on the back nine. One shot. When your mind drifts, use your post-shot routine to let go and reset.

After the round

Rate yourself 1–10 on the focus you picked. That number tells you as much as your score. Mental game coaching at Under Par tracks exactly this: see mindset coaching.
