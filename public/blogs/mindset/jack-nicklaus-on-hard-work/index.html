Jack Nicklaus on Hard Work and Confidence

Under Par Coaching Team · Updated November 27, 2026 · 2 min read

Jack Nicklaus called confidence the most important single factor in golf, and said there's only one way to get it and keep it: work. Talent helps. It doesn't replace the hours.

That's true off the course too. Results usually reflect the effort behind them. Here's how to make room for the work.

1. Rank your goals

Most of us juggle family, friends, hobbies and a career. You can't give everything 100%. Write your goals down and rank them by how much they matter to you, golf included.

2. Build a weekly and daily agenda

Map out a normal week and see where the hours actually go. Then decide what to cut and what gets more time. If golf is high on the list, practice goes on the calendar like a meeting. Need a structure? Start with the 30-minute practice plan.

3. Expect interruptions

A family member needs help. A work trip appears. Stick to the plan as closely as you can, accept the detours, and don't let them become excuses.

Then get to work. For more, read setting your golf goals and what the GOATs have in common.
