4 Keys to Achieving Your Golf Goals

Under Par Coaching Team · Updated November 27, 2026 · 2 min read

Setting goals is the easy part. Reaching them takes a system. Whether you're new to the game or chasing scratch, these four keys turn a list of goals into real progress. (Haven't set them yet? Start with the do's and don'ts of golf goals.)

1. Write them down where you'll see them

A note on your phone works. The point is to see them often, so they stay in front of you all season.

2. Build an action plan for each goal

For every goal, list the steps to get there and the resources you'll need: coaching, practice time, equipment, training aids. "Make 80% of putts from 3 feet" becomes "putting routine three times a week" plus a gate drill.

3. Set up accountability

Few golfers hold themselves accountable consistently, and every season has ups and downs. Identify the people who'll push you back to practice when motivation drops: a playing partner, a coach, a group chat.

4. Do the work, and make it fun

Big goals take sustained effort. Find ways to enjoy it: games, challenges, competing with friends, beating last week's score.

Learn from the tour

Tour players' goals are heavily statistical, because a single stroke per round is worth a fortune at that level. Justin Thomas has shared his season goals publicly, and they read like a stats sheet. Your strokes may not be worth that much, but stat goals work the same way for you. And like tour players, it helps to have someone checking in on your progress. That's what an Under Par coach does.
