Roll 'Em In: A 60-Minute Putting Session for Holing More Putts

Under Par Coaching Team · Updated November 27, 2026 · 2 min read

This session is about making putts, plus a short block of bump-and-runs to set them up.

Mindset: don't avoid big tournaments because of nerves. Golfers who keep putting themselves in pressure spots get more comfortable in them.

The session at a glance

Block Time Reps Score
1. Putter path drill 8 min 18 Smooth along the line
2. 5-footers 14 min 27 Makes out of 27
3. 10-footers 14 min 27 Makes out of 27
4. Long range 15 min 18 Makes and three-putts
5. Bump and run 12 min 18 Inside 6 feet

1. Putter path drill (8 min)

A straight 5-footer with an alignment stick parallel to the line, just outside the ball. 18 putts.

2. 5-footers (14 min)

Nine straight, nine breaking about 3 inches left to right, nine breaking about 3 inches right to left. Count makes for each.

3. 10-footers (14 min)

Same structure, with about 5 inches of break each way.

4. Long range (15 min)

Three balls from 25, 40 and 55 feet, holing out every one. Twice through, 18 putts. Track makes and three-putts.

5. Bump and run (12 min)

Three shots each from 20, 30 and 40 yards. Then repeat with a less-lofted club: most golfers use too much loft, and the goal is to get the ball rolling on the green quickly. Note which club is more consistent at each distance.
