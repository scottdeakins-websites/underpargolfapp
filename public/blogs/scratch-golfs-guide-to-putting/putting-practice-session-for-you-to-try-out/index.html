Flat Stick Day: A 66-Minute Putting Practice Session

Under Par Coaching Team · Updated November 27, 2026 · 2 min read

A full day on the practice green, because that's where a huge share of your strokes happen.

Mindset: obstacles are part of the path. Work through them.

The session at a glance

Block Time Putts Score
1. Circle drill 10 min 24 Makes out of 24
2. 15-20-25 footers 30 min 30 Makes (beat 3)
3. Lag putting 20 min 30 Inside 3 feet
4. Tiger's gate drill 6 min 18 Clean through

1. Circle drill (10 min)

Three balls at 2, 3 and 4 feet, at eight spots around the hole. 24 putts, count your makes.

2. 15-20-25 footers (30 min)

One putt each from 15, 20 and 25 feet, holing out every one. Ten circuits, 30 putts. Can you make more than three? Count your three-putts too. The goal is zero.

3. Lag putting (20 min)

Three stations at 40, 50 and 60 feet with three balls each, plus a tenth ball to finish back at 50 feet. Three rounds, 30 putts. Score how many finish inside 3 feet.

4. Tiger's gate drill (6 min)

From 3 feet, two tees just wider than your putter head. 12 putts with your lead hand only, then 6 with both.
