Accuracy Challenge: A 75-Minute Full Swing Practice Session

Under Par Coaching Team · Updated November 27, 2026 · 2 min read

Accuracy under pressure comes from practice that has consequences. This session builds a six-stage challenge where you have to hit back-to-back good shots to move on.

The session at a glance

Block Time Balls Score
1. Warm-up 15 min 29 No score
2. Accuracy challenge 50 min 50 Balls used / stages cleared
3. Weak link 10 min 15 No score

1. Warm-up (15 min)

Use the pre-round warm-up: change targets on every full shot and finish with three balls from the first tee, with your full routine.

2. The accuracy challenge (50 min)

You have 50 balls to clear six stages in order. To clear a stage, hit two in a row into the target. Miss and the count resets.

Stage Shot Target
1 Driver 25-yard-wide fairway
2 3-wood 25-yard-wide fairway
3 Hybrid or long iron 25-yard-wide fairway
4 Approach A 100-yard "green," about 20 yards across
5 Approach A 150-yard green
6 Approach A 200-yard green

Score: total balls used (a perfect run is 12) or how far you got. Finish early? Use the leftover balls for a second run. Repeat this challenge for three weeks and track the trend.

3. Weak link (10 min)

Which stage cost you the most? Hit 15 balls on exactly that shot, same club, same target, to get ready for next week's attempt.

Pairs well with the fairway finder for stages 1–3.
