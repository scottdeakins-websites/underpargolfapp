The 40-Minute Pre-Round Golf Warm-Up

Under Par Coaching Team · Updated November 27, 2026 · 2 min read

A good warm-up isn't practice. You're not fixing anything; you're finding your speed, rhythm and confidence for the day. This one takes about 40 minutes. Trim the full swing block if you're short on time.

1. Putting (10–15 min)

Start on the practice green to read the speed.

  • 10 putts from 3 feet
  • From 10 feet: one uphill, one downhill, one flat
  • From 25 feet: one uphill, one downhill, one flat
  • 3 long lag putts, focused only on distance

2. Short game (5–10 min)

  • 3 pitches of about 20 yards and 3 of about 30 yards from the fairway
  • Chips from the rough at 10, 15 and 20 yards
  • 3 bunker shots

3. Full swing (15–25 min)

Work up through the bag, with a new target for every full shot.

Club Balls Notes
Pitching wedge 5 75% effort
Pitching wedge 5 Full, different target each
8-iron 5 Full, different target each
8-iron 3 75% effort
5-iron 5 Full, different target each
Fairway wood 3 At a 15-yard-wide fairway
Driver 3 At a 15-yard-wide fairway
First-tee club 3 Visualize the opening shot

Rules for the warm-up

  • No swing changes. Dance with what you brought.
  • Don't chase a few bad ones. Stay calm and stick to the routine.
  • Use your full routine on the last 10 balls.
