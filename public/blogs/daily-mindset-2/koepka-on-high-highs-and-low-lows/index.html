Daily Mindset: Brooks Koepka on the Highs and Lows

Under Par Coaching Team · Updated November 27, 2026 · 2 min read

Brooks Koepka has described golf this way: when you're playing well, you feel like you'll never lose it, and when you're playing badly, you feel like you'll never find it again.

Golf has high highs and low lows. A good stretch makes you think you've found the secret. A bad one makes you think you'll never get out of the rut. Every golfer goes through both, including the best players in the world.

The way through is the same every time: patience, work and belief in yourself, even when your scores aren't showing it yet.
