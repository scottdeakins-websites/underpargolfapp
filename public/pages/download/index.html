4 free practice sessions you can print

Real results come from real training. These are four sessions from the Under Par coaching team, one for each part of the game. To keep a copy, use your browser's Print → Save as PDF.

1. Full-swing accuracy challenge (20 min)

Drill Reps Score
Set two flags about 30 yards apart as a fairway. Hit drivers. Until you miss Longest streak inside the flags
Pick a small target, hit 10 drivers 10 Average miss, in yards
5 × 8-iron, 5 × 5-iron at a flag 10 Shots finishing inside 10 yards

2. Wedge accuracy and distance (25 min)

Drill Reps Score
Each wedge: full grip, 1 inch down, 2 inches down 3 each Write down every carry distance
Full wedges to 80, 90, 100, 110 yards 5 each Shots within 5 yards of the number

Your carry numbers become your wedge distance chart.

3. Short game challenge (30 min)

Drill Reps Score
Chips from the rough: 10, 15, 20, 25 yards 5 each Inside 6 feet
Pitches from the fairway: 20, 30, 40, 50 yards 5 each Inside 10 feet
Bunker: 10, 15, 20, 25 yards 2 each Out and on the green

4. Short and mid-range putting games (25 min)

Drill Reps Score
Circle drill: 3 balls at 2, 3, 4 ft, around the hole in 8 spots 24 Makes out of 24
6, 9, 12, 25 ft, breaking both ways 24 Makes out of 24
Coin drill: roll over a marker 2 ft ahead from 25 ft 18 Hits out of 18
