Founder and CEO / Coach

Scott Deakins

Coach Scott started Under Par to combine his passions for software and golf. He takes a holistic approach, starting with a player's goals and working backward, and helps golfers with every part of their game, from swing mechanics to pre-shot routine to practice regimen.

Golf Performance Coach

John Guillaume

John started golf at 9 and won the Washington State Junior Championship at 11, plus dozens of regional titles as a junior. He stepped away for a career in technology before coming back to the game. A self-proclaimed golf nerd who competes on the senior circuit, John emphasizes natural athletic movement and keeps training as simple as possible.

Mental Coach

Michael is a full-time writer, author, creator of Wicked Smart Golf and a +1 handicap amateur. He left a national corporate sales career in 2017 to pursue entrepreneurship and professional golf, and has since played 160+ tournament days, including Q-school in 2019.
