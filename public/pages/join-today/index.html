Questions

What happens after I sign up?

Complete the player survey: your goals, your game and how much time you have. Your coach uses it to build your first week of practice and workouts in the Under Par app.

What is the Go Low Guarantee?

Work with your coach for six months. If you haven't cut at least two strokes off your handicap, your next six months are free.

How much time do I need?

Plans work whether you have one day a week or seven, including home and indoor sessions.

Do I need a low handicap?

No. Your plan targets your next milestone: break 100, 90, 80 or par.
