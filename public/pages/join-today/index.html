Questions

How much does it cost?

$39 per month. That covers unlimited coaching, your practice plan, your workout plan and the guarantee.

What is the Go Low Guarantee?

Work with your coach for six months. If you haven't cut at least two strokes off your handicap, your next six months are free.

How much time do I need?

Plans work whether you have one day a week or seven. Your coach builds around the time you really have, including home and indoor sessions.

Do I need a low handicap?

No. Your plan targets your next milestone: break 100, 90, 80 or par.

How do I talk to my coach?

Through the Under Par app inbox. Post practice results, swing videos and round stats, and your coach responds there.
