Questions

Does the $39 plan include a coach?

No. The App Plan ($39/month or $374/year) gives you structured practice and golf fitness plans in the Under Par app. A personal coach is part of Full Access ($199/month), which is currently full.

How do I get on the Full Access waitlist?

Fill out the waitlist form. A coach will be in touch once a spot opens up.

What happens after I sign up for the App Plan?

Complete the player survey about your game and goals, then get started with your practice and fitness plans in the Under Par app.

What is the Go Low Guarantee?

It's part of Full Access coaching. After six months working with an Under Par coach, if you haven't cut at least two strokes off your handicap, your next six months are free.

How much time do I need?

Plans work whether you have one day a week or seven, including home and indoor sessions.
