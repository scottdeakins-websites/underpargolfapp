Our mission

The coach we wish we'd had

Scott Deakins grew up loving golf, but coaching was hard to come by. Most of what he learned came from trial and error. Under Par is the coaching platform he wanted back then.

For players

A modern way to work with a coach on everything from your swing to your mindset, with resources to get a little better every day. It shouldn't matter where you live or what facilities you have nearby.

For golf professionals

More time coaching and less time chasing range bookings. Recurring coaching income that goes to the pro directly, a better work-life balance, and a closer connection with the players they coach.

Ready to Go Low?

Structured practice and golf fitness plans in the Under Par app, from $39 a month. Want a personal coach? Full Access coaching ($199/month) is currently full, so join the waitlist.
