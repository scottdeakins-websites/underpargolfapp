Bad decisions cost more strokes than bad swings. Strategy coaching is where you pick those strokes up.

Course management tools

Building a yardage book, using a rangefinder well, reading greens, picking the right side of the tee box, and knowing which pins to attack.

Post-round reviews

After each round, you and your coach go through where shots were lost. Did a strategy change save strokes? Where should next week's practice go? The plan updates from there.

Game plans for big rounds

Before a tournament or a big match: a practice plan for the lead-up, a yardage book for the course, where to be aggressive and where to play safe, and how to adjust for weather.
