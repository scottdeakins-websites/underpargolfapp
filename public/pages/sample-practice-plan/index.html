Free practice plan

A free 4-day golf practice plan

This is the same structure as our app practice plans, built for any skill level. Each day links to a full session with drills, rep counts and a way to score yourself. Do them in order, keep your scores, and repeat the week to see what moved.

Day Session Focus Time
1 The 1-hour practice session Putting, wedges and the long game 60 min
2 Landing Zone Chipping and pitching to a spot 40 min
3 Putting Speed Control Start line, contact and lag putting 50 min
4 Save-Par Short Game Up-and-downs and bunker play 84 min

Make it yours

The sample week is balanced across the whole game. A real plan isn't. If you're losing six shots a round around the greens, that's where your week goes. With Full Access, your coach reads your stats, picks the sessions and changes them as your scores change. The App Plan (from $39/month) gives you structured weekly plans to follow.

Short on time?

Swap Day 4 for the fairway finder drill or a round of flighted wedges. Twenty focused minutes beats an hour of hitting balls without a target.
