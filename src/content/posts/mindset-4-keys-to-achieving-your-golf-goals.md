Setting goals is the easy part. Reaching them takes a system. Whether you're new to the game or chasing scratch, these four keys turn a list of goals into real progress. (Haven't set them yet? Start with [the do's and don'ts of golf goals](/blogs/mindset/setting-your-annual-golf-goals-the-do-s-and-don-ts).)

## 1. Write them down where you'll see them

A note on your phone works. The point is to see them often, so they stay in front of you all season.

## 2. Build an action plan for each goal

For every goal, list the steps to get there and the resources you'll need: coaching, practice time, equipment, training aids. "Make 80% of putts from 3 feet" becomes "putting routine three times a week" plus a [gate drill](/blogs/scratch-golfs-guide-to-putting/putting-practice-routine).

## 3. Set up accountability

Few golfers hold themselves accountable consistently, and every season has ups and downs. Identify the people who'll push you back to practice when motivation drops: a playing partner, a coach, a group chat.

## 4. Do the work, and make it fun

Big goals take sustained effort. Find ways to enjoy it: games, challenges, competing with friends, beating last week's score.
