Practice is easy when nothing's on the line. This session puts something on the line, so your short game holds up when it matters.

## The session at a glance

| Block | Time | Reps | Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Hurricane putting | 20 min | 40 | Makes out of 40 |
| 2. Lag work | 8 min | 15 | Inside 2 feet |
| 3. Chipping | 25 min | 30 | Inside 5 feet |
| 4. Career-low challenge | 9 min | 3 holes | Total strokes |

## 1. Hurricane putting (20 min)

Place five balls in a spiral around the hole at 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 feet, each on a different side, so every putt has a different break and pace. Try to make all five. Eight rounds, 40 putts. Count your makes.

## 2. Lag work (8 min)

Three balls at 30, 40 and 50 feet. Leave each inside 2 feet; holing it is a bonus. Five rounds, 15 putts.

## 3. Chipping (25 min)

From the rough, five chips each to targets at 10, 15 and 20 yards. Twice through: 30 chips. Score how many finish inside 5 feet.

## 4. The career-low challenge (9 min)

You need to get up and down on the last three holes to shoot your career low. You've missed every green. Play each one out:

1. A 20-yard chip from the rough
2. A 15-yard bunker shot
3. A 25-yard bump and run

**8 strokes** ties your career low. **7** beats it. Write down the score.
