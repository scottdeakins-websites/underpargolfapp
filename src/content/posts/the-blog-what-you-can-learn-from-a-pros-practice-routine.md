You don't have a tour player's coaches, facilities or free time. But you can copy *how* they practice. Here are six habits worth stealing.

## 1. Putting practice is drill-based

Pros don't just roll putts at a hole. Every putting session targets a specific skill:

| Skill | Example from tour |
|---|---|
| Eye line | Justin Thomas uses an alignment mirror |
| Putter path | Jordan Spieth works with an alignment rod |
| Square face | Tiger Woods' gate of tees |
| Start line | Rolling putts along a chalk line or string |

All of these are in our [putting practice routine](/blogs/scratch-golfs-guide-to-putting/putting-practice-routine).

## 2. Most of the time goes to short game

Pros move around the short game area simulating shots they'll actually face. Three drills to try:

- **Three trajectories:** three balls to the same target, each with a different flight.
- **Bump and runs:** three bump-and-runs, each with a different club.
- **Nine random holes:** play nine holes around the chipping green with one ball, holing out every time. Track your best nine-hole score.

## 3. Range sessions vary every shot

You never hit the same shot twice in a row on the course, so don't on the range.

- **Distance control:** change your target on every swing, or hit two different clubs to the same flag.
- **Shot shape:** the 9-ball drill: three draws, three fades and three straight shots. Count how many come off.
- **Trajectory:** practice high, normal and low with the same club, for wind and for taking distance off.

## 4. They know their numbers

Tour players live on stats, and some hire statisticians. You don't need to: track fairways, greens, up-and-downs and putts in an app like 18Birdies, Golfshot or SwingU. Then give your weakest area the most practice time.

## 5. They don't ignore their strengths

Most amateurs only practice what's broken. Pros keep their strengths sharp too, because a strength that slips costs as many strokes as a weakness.

## 6. They plan and track everything

At minimum: plan your week, track every result, set a weekly challenge and keep a practice journal. Start with [how to build a golf practice plan](/blogs/the-blog/golf-practice-plan).
