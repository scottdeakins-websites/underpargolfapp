Not long ago, tour players regularly left the driver in the bag for irons and fairway woods. Tiger's famous stinger was part of that era. Today, they hit driver much more often. Two reasons:

1. **Data.** Strokes-gained analysis showed that, for most players, extra distance is worth more than the accuracy given up. A short iron from the rough often beats a mid-iron from the fairway.
2. **Equipment.** Modern drivers are extremely forgiving on off-center hits.

## Has your game caught up?

If you trust your driver, try a round where you hit it on every hole where it's reasonable: aggressive off the tee, sensible after that.

## When to hold back

- **Penalty trouble in your landing zone:** out of bounds or water where your normal miss goes.
- **The fairway pinches** exactly at your driver distance.
- **You're not confident with it yet.**

## Not confident? Build it first

- Use the **[fairway finder](/blogs/full-swing-guide-from-scratch-golf/3-steps-to-building-a-fairway-finder-off-the-tee)**: tee it lower and choke down an inch or two. It's still usually longer than 3-wood.
- **Tee up on the right side** of the box for your shot shape: [here's how](/blogs/playing-strategies-for-golfers/3-quick-tips-to-optimizing-angles-off-the-tee).
- **Practice the fairway streak drill** until you've got a number you trust.

Then let it fly.
