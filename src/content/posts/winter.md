The off-season is when good golfers get better. There's no score to protect, so you can make the [swing change](/blogs/full-swing-guide-from-scratch-golf/the-art-of-making-swing-changes) you've been putting off, get stronger and build exact yardages. Here's a weekly plan for winter, whether you have a simulator, a garage net or just a hallway.

## The winter week

| Day | Session | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Golf strength workout | Gym or home |
| Tue | Putting at home (20 min) | Living room / putting mat |
| Wed | Simulator or net: technique block | Indoor |
| Thu | Golf strength workout | Gym or home |
| Sat | Simulator: yardages and target games | Indoor |
| Sun | Mobility and putting (20 min) | Home |

## Putting at home (20 min)

- **Gate drill (5 min):** two coins or tees just wider than your putter head, 18 putts.
- **Start line (10 min):** putt along a seam in the floor or an alignment stick, trying to roll the ball straight for 6–8 feet.
- **Distance ladder (5 min):** putt to targets at 10, 15 and 20 feet on a mat or carpet. Stop each one within a putter length.

## Simulator and net sessions

**Technique block (45 min).** Work on one swing change, one thought only. Half to three-quarter speed for the first 15 balls, then full speed. Film from face-on and down the line every 10 balls.

**Yardages (first session, 45 min).** Hit 5–7 full shots with every club, throw out the mishits, and record your average carry and your max. That's your [yardage guide](/blogs/full-swing-guide-from-scratch-golf/building-a-yardage-guide). Repeat for [flighted wedges](/blogs/full-swing-guide-from-scratch-golf/the-art-of-hitting-flighted-wedges).

**Target games (30 min).** Pick a target and hit 10 balls with three different clubs. Score the average distance from the target. Simulators are perfect for this: they measure it for you.

**Play a round.** Simulate a course you'll play in the spring. Use your full routine on every shot and keep score honestly.

## The gym

Winter is when you build the strength and mobility that turn into speed in April. Focus on the [5 muscle groups golfers need](/blogs/fitness-for-golfers/5-muscle-groups-to-focus-on-to-maximize-your-golf-fitness) two or three days a week, plus a daily mobility routine for hips and shoulders.

## When spring comes

Take the swing change to the course in casual rounds first, and switch to a scoring plan like the [break 80](/blogs/the-blog/break-80-practice-plan) or [break 90](/blogs/the-blog/break-90-practice-plan) program.
