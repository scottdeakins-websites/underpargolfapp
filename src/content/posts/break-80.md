Golfers in the low 80s can usually hit the ball. What separates them from the 70s is **wasted shots inside 120 yards**: a wedge that comes up short, a chip to 15 feet, a 40-footer that turns into three putts.

So this plan spends most of its time close to the green, and keeps the driver accurate.

## Where the strokes are

| Area | Goal for breaking 80 |
|---|---|
| Tee shot | Fairway or first cut, almost no penalties |
| Wedges | Known yardages for every wedge, inside 20 feet from 100 yards |
| Short game | Get up and down regularly from easy lies |
| Putting | Lag to 3 feet, make nearly everything inside 4 |

## The weekly schedule

Three sessions and one round. About 3½ hours of practice.

| Day | Session | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Session 1 | [Save-par short game session](/blogs/short-game-improvement-guide/short-game-practice-session-to-help-you-save-par) | 84 min |
| Session 2 | Wedges and driver (below) | 60 min |
| Session 3 | [Putting speed control](/blogs/the-blog/sample-practice-session-putting-speed-control) | 50 min |
| Weekend | Play 18, log fairways, GIR, up-and-downs, putts | — |

## Session 2: Wedges and driver (60 min)

**1. Build your wedge chart (first week only, 20 min).** Hit each wedge at full length, choked down 1 inch and choked down 2 inches. Write down the carries. Full method: [wedge distance control](/blogs/full-swing-guide-from-scratch-golf/how-to-manage-wedge-distances-like-a-pro).

**2. Wedge ladder (25 min).** Five shots each to 70, 85, 100 and 115 yards using your chart. Score: within 5 yards of the number. Use [flighted wedges](/blogs/full-swing-guide-from-scratch-golf/the-art-of-hitting-flighted-wedges) for at least half.

**3. Fairway streak (15 min).** Two flags as a fairway, driver until you miss. Score: best streak.

## What to do on the course

- **Know three numbers on every approach:** front, pin and back. Your [yardage guide](/blogs/full-swing-guide-from-scratch-golf/building-a-yardage-guide) makes this easy.
- **Use the tee box.** [Tee up on the side](/blogs/playing-strategies-for-golfers/3-quick-tips-to-optimizing-angles-off-the-tee) that fits your shot shape.
- **Attack only with a wedge in hand.** From mid-irons, play to the fat part of the green.
