This session builds accuracy under pressure, and gives you a warm-up routine you can reuse before every round. You'll need a full bag and about 40 balls for the challenge.

## The session at a glance

| Block | Time | Balls | Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Full swing warm-up | 12 min | 18 | No score |
| 2. Target challenge | 30 min | Up to 40 | Balls used to finish the bag |
| 3. Weak link | 3 min | 5 | No score |

## 1. Full swing warm-up (12 min)

Three balls at each step. From step 2 on, pick a new specific target for every shot.

| Step | Club | Target |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pitching wedge at 50% | Easy rhythm |
| 2 | Pitching wedge, full | Specific target |
| 3 | 8-iron | Specific target |
| 4 | 5-iron | Specific target |
| 5 | Fairway wood | A 15-yard-wide section of fairway |
| 6 | Driver | A 15-yard-wide section of fairway |

Use this exact sequence before rounds too.

## 2. Target challenge (30 min)

- Pick a target **15 yards wide**.
- Start with your highest-lofted club. Once you hit the target, move to the next club, working up to the driver.
- You have **40 balls** to get through the bag.
- Finish under 40? Go back through the bag again with what's left.

**Score:** the number of balls it took. Write it down and beat it next time. Every ball counts, so every swing gets your full pre-shot routine.

## 3. Weak link (3 min)

Which club cost you the most balls? Hit five more with it to finish on a good note.
