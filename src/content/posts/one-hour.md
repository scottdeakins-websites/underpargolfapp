You have an hour. Most golfers spend it hitting a large bucket of drivers. This plan spends it the way your scores are actually made: most of the time inside 110 yards.

Every block has a rep count and a score. Write the scores down. Next week, you're trying to beat them.

## The plan at a glance

| Block | Time | Reps | Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Circle drill | 10 min | 24 putts | Makes out of 24 |
| 2. Mid-range putts | 10 min | 24 putts | Makes out of 24 |
| 3. Chipping from the rough | 10 min | 20 shots | Inside 6 feet |
| 4. Pitching from the fairway | 10 min | 20 shots | Inside 10 feet |
| 5. Full wedges | 10 min | 20 shots | Within 5 yards of the number |
| 6. Irons | 5 min | 10 shots | Inside 10 yards of the flag |
| 7. Driver | 5 min | 10 shots | Average miss in yards |

**Total: 60 minutes, 80 shots, 48 putts.**

## Putting: 20 minutes

**1. Circle drill (10 min).** Place balls at 2, 3 and 4 feet from the hole. Putt all three, then move 45 degrees around the hole and repeat, until you've gone all the way around. That's 24 putts. Count your makes.

**2. Mid-range putts (10 min).** Find a putt that breaks left to right. Hit three balls each from 6, 9, 12 and 25 feet. Then find one breaking right to left and do it again. 24 putts, count the makes. If the 25-footers are finishing more than three feet away, add our [speed control session](/blogs/the-blog/sample-practice-session-putting-speed-control) to your week.

## Wedges: 30 minutes

**3. Chipping (10 min).** Five shots each from 10, 15, 20 and 25 yards, all from the rough. Change the lie every time: sitting up, sitting down, into the grain. The course doesn't give you the same lie twice.

**4. Pitching (10 min).** Five shots each from 20, 30, 40 and 50 yards, from the fairway. For an extra challenge, hit each one at a different trajectory: low, medium, high.

**5. Full wedges (10 min).** Five shots each to 80, 90, 100 and 110 yards. You're scoring distance, not direction. A wedge that finishes eight yards long and on line is still a miss. If you don't know your wedge numbers yet, [build a wedge chart first](/blogs/full-swing-guide-from-scratch-golf/how-to-manage-wedge-distances-like-a-pro).

## Range: 10 minutes

**6. Irons (5 min).** Five 8-irons and five 5-irons at a flag. Full routine on every shot.

**7. Driver (5 min).** Pick a small target, like a flag or a distant tree, and hit 10 drivers. Estimate how far each one misses, in yards, and take the average. Want to tighten that number? Try the [fairway finder](/blogs/full-swing-guide-from-scratch-golf/3-steps-to-building-a-fairway-finder-off-the-tee).

## Why this split works

Most of the shots in your round happen inside 110 yards, so that's where most of your practice should go. The range blocks are short on purpose: ten focused swings with a target and a routine teach you more than fifty without one.
