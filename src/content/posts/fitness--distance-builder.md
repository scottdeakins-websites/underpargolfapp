This is a sample from the Under Par golf workout plan: a shoulder strength and power day. Expect about an hour. *Check with a doctor or physical therapist before starting a new program, and scale weights to your level.*

## 1. Yoga flow

Three rounds, 60 seconds on each:

- Child's pose
- Bird dog
- Cat-cow
- Cobra
- Tabletop twist

## 2. Warm-up

Three fairly quick rounds of 8 reps each:

- Burpees
- Jumping air squats
- Light dumbbell push press
- Light dumbbell front raise
- Light dumbbell lateral raise

## 3. Strength: shoulder press

**5 sets of 5** at about 70% of your one-rep max. Build up with 3–5 lighter warm-up sets first.

## 4. Conditioning: EMOM (25 min)

Five rounds. Each round is five minutes:

| Minute | Exercise |
|---|---|
| 1 | Max box jumps (or box step-ups) |
| 2 | Max dumbbell push press |
| 3 | Max dumbbell goblet squats |
| 4 | Max plank hold |
| 5 | Rest |

**Dumbbell weights (each hand):**

| Level | Men | Women |
|---|---|---|
| Beginner | 20 lb | 10 lb |
| Intermediate | 35 lb | 25 lb |
| Advanced | 50 lb | 35 lb |

## Coaching notes

- **Test run:** do two rounds at 30 seconds per movement first, to find your rhythm and check your weights.
- **Push hard:** the rest minute is built in.
- **Stay consistent:** try to keep your reps within about 20% from round 1 to round 5.
