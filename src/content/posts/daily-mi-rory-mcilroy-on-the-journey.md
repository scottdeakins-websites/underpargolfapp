> "It's not always about the results, it's about the journey."
> — often attributed to Rory McIlroy

If you only measure golf by trophies and scores, you miss most of what makes it worth doing. The progress, the setbacks and what you learn from them matter as much as the final number. Obstacles are where resilience gets built.

**Today:** pick one process goal, like your full [pre-shot routine](/blogs/mindset/a-simple-blueprint-for-your-pre-shot-routine) on every shot, and judge your round by that, not by the score.
