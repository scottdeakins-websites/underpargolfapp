Progress comes from small daily choices: the extra five putts, the practice session on a windy day, staying focused on the course. This session is one of those choices. It's long, and it covers everything you need to get up and down.

## The session at a glance

| Block | Time | Reps | Score |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Chipping: three trajectories | 20 min | 27 | No score |
| 2. Bunker line drill | 8 min | 20 | Sand entry at the line |
| 3. 18-hole chipping challenge | 25 min | 18 holes | Total strokes (target 36) |
| 4. Impossible shots | 8 min | 5+ | Just for fun |
| 5. Clock drill | 15 min | 24 | Makes out of 24 |
| 6. Speed practice | 12 min | 12+ | Pass each distance |

## 1. Chipping: three trajectories (20 min)

From 10, 20 and 30 yards, hit three chips each: normal, high and low. Three sets, 27 shots.

## 2. Bunker line drill (8 min)

Draw a line in the sand. Make your swing enter the sand right at the line, every time. 20 reps. It's the most useful bunker drill there is, at any level.

## 3. 18-hole chipping challenge (25 min)

Play 18 "holes" around the chipping green. Drop a ball, bounce it off your club three times, and play it as it lies. Chip and putt out. **Target: 36** (two per hole). Green crowded? Count chips that finish inside 5 feet instead.

## 4. Impossible shots (8 min)

Have fun with five shots you hope never to face: a plugged lie in a bunker, a ball in a divot, a downslope in a bunker, an awkward lie in the rough, and a flop to a tucked pin. More on why in [practice from bad lies](/blogs/the-blog/practice-tip-practice-from-bad-lies).

## 5. Clock drill (15 min)

Three balls at 2, 3 and 4 feet, at eight positions around the hole: 24 putts. Phil Mickelson reportedly won't leave the green until he's made all of them.

## 6. Speed practice (12 min)

Lay a club 2 feet behind the hole. From 10 feet, hit three putts: each must finish in the hole or between the hole and the club. Pass, then move to 20, 30 and 40 feet.
