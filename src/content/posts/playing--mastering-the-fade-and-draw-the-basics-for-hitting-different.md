Tour players curve the ball to get around obstacles, use the wind and position it on the green. How much you should practice shaping shots depends on your level. For most golfers, it's more important to [own one shape](/blogs/playing-strategies-for-golfers/how-to-manage-your-game-like-a-pga-player) than to hit both. But knowing how to curve it either way gets you out of trouble.

*Directions are for a right-handed golfer. Lefties, mirror them.*

## The setup at a glance

| | Fade (curves right) | Draw (curves left) |
|---|---|---|
| Body alignment | Left of target | Right of target |
| Clubface | Open to your body line, near the target | Closed to your body line, near the target |
| Ball position | Slightly forward | Slightly back |
| Swing path | Along your body line: out-to-in to the target | Along your body line: in-to-out to the target |
| Typical flight | A little higher, softer landing | A little lower, more roll |

## How to hit a fade

1. Aim your feet, hips and shoulders left of the target.
2. Set the clubface pointing at (or just left of) the target, which is open relative to your body.
3. Play the ball slightly forward.
4. Swing along your body line. Relative to the target, that's out-to-in, and the open face sends it curving back right.

## How to hit a draw

1. Aim your body right of the target.
2. Set the clubface pointing at (or just right of) the target, which is closed relative to your body.
3. Play the ball slightly back.
4. Swing along your body line, in-to-out relative to the target, and the ball curves back left.

## How to practice

- **Block practice:** spend a session on the shape that doesn't come naturally.
- **The 9-shot drill:** fade, draw and straight at three heights with one club. On a simulator, it measures your face and path for you. See [simulator practice strategies](/blogs/the-blog/3-strategies-to-maximize-your-golf-simulator-practice).
- **On course:** use it to get around trouble. For most players, from the fairway, play your stock shape.

A coach can check your face and path on video and tell you which shape to build your game around: that's part of [swing coaching](/pages/swing-coaching).
