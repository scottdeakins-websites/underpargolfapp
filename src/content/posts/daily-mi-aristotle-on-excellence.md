> "Excellence is never an accident. It is always the result of high intention, sincere effort, and intelligent execution."
> — often attributed to Aristotle

We see the trophies, the green jackets and the finished products. We rarely see what went into them: the early mornings, the missed family events, the long hours. Tiger Woods, Annika Sorenstam, Serena Williams and Kobe Bryant all paid that price. So did Steve Jobs and Bill Gates in business.

**Working hard isn't enough. Work smart:** review your performance, ask for feedback, and adjust your approach over time. That's what a [golf practice plan](/blogs/the-blog/golf-practice-plan) is for.

Excellence is a choice. Make it today.
