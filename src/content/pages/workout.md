A better turn and more speed usually start in the gym. Your Under Par workout plan is built for golf, and it fits around the rest of your week.

## The schedule

Plan on **4–6 days a week, about 30–75 minutes** each. Move days around whenever you need to. Workouts live in the Under Par app next to your practice plan, and your coach sees your results as you log them.

## What it trains

| Muscle group | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Back | Holding your spine angle through the swing |
| Upper legs | Speed and power from the ground up |
| Hips and shoulders (smaller muscles) | A fuller, more repeatable turn |

## How it's structured

- Percentage-based strength work
- Monthly training cycles
- Fitness challenges and benchmarks
- Guided warm-ups and stretching
- Golf-specific accessory work
- Demo videos for every exercise
