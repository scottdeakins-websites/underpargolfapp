Your practice plan, workouts, drills and results all live in one place. Coach features (inbox and feedback) are part of [Full Access](/pages/wait-list).

## The tabs

| Tab | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Today** | Your daily mindset, today's practice and workout, and tasks from your coach (Full Access) |
| **Coaching** | Your full practice and fitness plans, plus articles on mindset, strategy, full swing, short game and putting |
| **On-Demand** | Extra sessions, and **Play a Round** to log round details (sent to your coach with Full Access) |
| **Inbox** | Direct messages with your coach (Full Access) |
| **You** | Your goals and personal details |

## Your daily loop

1. **Practice.** Mark sessions complete, log scores and comments, or reschedule from the training page.
2. **Work out.** Complete the day's fitness work, log results and track personal bests.
3. **Play.** Record fairways, greens in regulation, putts and up-and-downs, or send a screenshot from your stat-tracking app.
4. **Get feedback (Full Access).** When your coach replies, a notification appears in the top right.
