# A free 4-day golf practice plan

This is the structure our coaches use for members, built for any skill level. Each day links to a full session with drills, rep counts and a way to score yourself. Do them in order, keep your scores, and repeat the week to see what moved.

| Day | Session | Focus | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [The 1-hour practice session](/blogs/the-blog/best-1-hour-golf-practice-session) | Putting, wedges and the long game | 60 min |
| 2 | [Landing Zone](/blogs/the-blog/sample-practice-session-landing-zone) | Chipping and pitching to a spot | 40 min |
| 3 | [Putting Speed Control](/blogs/the-blog/sample-practice-session-putting-speed-control) | Start line, contact and lag putting | 50 min |
| 4 | [Save-Par Short Game](/blogs/short-game-improvement-guide/short-game-practice-session-to-help-you-save-par) | Up-and-downs and bunker play | 84 min |

## Make it yours

The sample week is balanced across the whole game. A real plan isn't. If you're losing six shots a round around the greens, that's where your week goes. Your Under Par coach reads your stats, picks the sessions and changes them as your scores change.

## Short on time?

Swap Day 4 for the [fairway finder drill](/blogs/full-swing-guide-from-scratch-golf/3-steps-to-building-a-fairway-finder-off-the-tee) or a round of [flighted wedges](/blogs/full-swing-guide-from-scratch-golf/the-art-of-hitting-flighted-wedges). Twenty focused minutes beats an hour of hitting balls without a target.
